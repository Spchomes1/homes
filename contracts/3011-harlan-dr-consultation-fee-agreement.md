## 6. Non-Circumvention

(a) For **twenty-four (24) months** after the Effective Date, neither Client nor any Covered Person shall acquire, contract to acquire, option or take title to the Subject Property except in a closing at which the full Consultation Fee is paid to Consultant. Nothing here stops Client from dealing directly with Seller — the obligation is to pay the fee if Client acquires the property.

(b) "**Covered Person**" means Client and its affiliates, principals, members, officers, employees, agents, family members, lenders, capital and joint-venture partners, assignees, nominees, entities under common control or formed by or for Client, and **anyone to whom Client discloses the Subject Property**. Client is responsible for their compliance.

(c) Without limiting subsection (a), this Section is triggered by closing in a different entity or name; letting a contract lapse and re-contracting within the period; restructuring or repricing with Seller so the fee is not paid in full; a person Client told about the deal acquiring it; or Client's lender or partner acquiring it instead of Client.

(d) On breach, the full Consultation Fee is **immediately due as a debt** for Services already rendered — not as liquidated damages or a penalty — with interest at the rate in Section 3(d), plus costs of collection and reasonable attorney's fees. Consultant may also seek injunctive relief. This Section survives closing and termination for its full term.

(e) Consultant will not record this Agreement or any memorandum or lien based on it in the real property records.

## 7. Confidentiality

Client shall keep the Seller's identity and contact information and the deal terms confidential and shall not disclose them except to its own advisors, lender and closing agent, who are bound by this Section.

## 8. If the Transaction Does Not Close

If Client's acquisition does not close for any reason **other than** Client's default or a breach of Section 6, no Consultation Fee is owed and Consultant has no claim for it. Consultant bears the risk that a sourced deal dies through no fault of Client. If closing fails because of Client's default or refusal to close on agreed terms, or if Section 6 is breached, the full Consultation Fee is immediately due as a debt under Section 6(d).
